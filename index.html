Power. Internet.
Education.

Make It So is a non-profit that brings solar power, Starlink internet, and streaming education to communities that have none of the three. Free. No strings attached.

Get Involved
Step 01

Solar

We set up solar power terminals so communities can generate their own electricity — no grid, no fuel runs, no waiting on anyone.

Step 02

Starlink

With power in place, we install Starlink satellite internet. A solar-powered terminal can be up and running in a few hours.

Step 03

Education

Once a community is online, we stream education — including homeschooling my own son — so kids learn more than they ever could before.

It's all free.

This is a non-profit. Nobody here is getting paid. If you can put me on a chopper and send me somewhere to set up a solar terminal and a Starlink dish, I'll spend a day or two putting it together — and then you have internet. Bye.

About

Ford Moore Jr. is a U.S. Air Force combat veteran of Operations Enduring Freedom and Iraqi Freedom, where he served four years as a Satcom and Ground Radio seven-level craftsman.

He then spent over ten years at the Bonneville Power Administration as a Power Systems Control Craftsman Two in the McNary District, maintaining the power grid of the Pacific Northwest. He medically retired in December 2024.

He now lives off-grid with his family, homeschools his son, and is building Make It So to bring the same self-reliance he built for himself to people who have neither power nor internet.
